Help

Welcome to Passgen. Choose how you want your passwords made. Everything you change here is saved straight away and becomes your default.

From now on, clicking the Passgen icon in the toolbar copies a new password made with these options to your clipboard. Nothing opens, and it is cleared again after the time set under Options. If you cannot see the icon, pin it from the puzzle piece menu in your browser. To come back here, right click the icon and choose Options.

Type or paste a password you already use to see a rough strength. It is checked on this computer only and is never saved or sent anywhere.

This is an estimate. It spots very common passwords, everyday words, years and simple runs of numbers, and otherwise treats the password as random, so it can still be a bit generous.
